Cashlio — the feedback list, screen by screen
Captured 26 Aug 2026 from the running app against a shop with real trade in it.

ADD PRODUCT
  01  Warranty in days/months/years, "Cut to length" for pipes and tubes,
      Brand as a controlled list, and the item-code rule stated on the form.
  02  A bad item code is refused as you type, naming the problem — it is not
      silently corrected, because the code goes on receipts and batch labels.

EDIT PRODUCT
  03  TYPO-ITEM-01 has no stock, so its item code is editable.
  04  ANC-SW-6A has stock, so the same field is locked.

ADD BATCH
  05  Purchase rate, the GST percentage on it, and whether the rate already
      includes GST — the split, on the way in.

DELETE PRODUCT
  06  Deleting a product that was never used asks first.
  07  And it is gone from the list. A product with stock is not offered a
      permanent delete at all — it can only be deactivated.

ADD CUSTOMER / ADD SUPPLIER
  08  Phone, email and GSTIN each refused under their own field, with the
      reason: "Enter a 10-digit mobile number (you entered 5 digits)."
  09  The same checks on the supplier form.

BILLING
  10  The dropdown lists every match, not just the first.
  11  Bill number INV-2608-xxxx in its own series, nothing like a batch code.
      Taxable value and Total are separate figures — GST is extracted from the
      price, not added to it.
  12  Cash short of the total: "Balance due", and the button reads
      "Collect ... on credit". No change is offered.
  13  UPI, the same.
  14  Card, the same.
  15  Overpaying still returns change — worded "Change to give back", not
      "change to return".

ANALYTICS
  16  Revenue, bill count, items sold, money collected by tender, and top
      products, all moving with the day's trade.

RETURN & REPLACEMENTS
  17  Sales history with credit notes (CN-) alongside sales, and PAID /
      PARTIAL / CREDIT / RETURN statuses.
  18  A credit note in detail.

CREDIT BALANCE & FOLLOW-UPS
  19  Who owes what, aged into buckets, with the follow-up notes that need
      chasing.

PLACE ORDER (PROCUREMENT)
  20  Orders in draft, placed and part-received, with value on order.
  21  An order in detail: ordered / received / pending per line, and the
      button that books goods in.
